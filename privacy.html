What Speedy List AI does with your data

  • When you analyze a photo, a resized copy of it is sent to our server (justaskmiggy.com) and to our AI provider, OpenAI, to identify the item and write the listing. We don't store your photos on our server; the result comes straight back to your phone.
  • Your listings, prices and the marketplaces you pick are saved only in your browser's local storage on your device.
  • Speedy List never asks for your marketplace passwords. Posting happens on each marketplace's own site, under your own login.
  • If you tap "Send to Miggy," your email app opens with your listing, and nothing is sent until you send it.
  • No ads or tracking are included.
