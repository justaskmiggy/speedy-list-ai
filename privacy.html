Privacy Policy Draft

Draft for the prototype - to be reviewed before any app-store submission. Last updated October 6, 2026.

What this prototype does with your data

  • Photos you drop or take stay on your device. The prototype does not upload them anywhere (the AI and posting are demos).
  • Marketplace "connections" are simulated and stored only in your browser's local storage (no passwords or tokens are collected).
  • No analytics, ads or tracking are included in the prototype.

When the full app launches

The production app will send your photos and listing details to our servers and AI provider to identify and price items, and to the marketplaces you choose to post to. Marketplace sign-ins will use each marketplace's official OAuth login; we will not see your marketplace passwords. This policy will be updated with full details (data collected, retention, third parties, deletion requests) before launch.
